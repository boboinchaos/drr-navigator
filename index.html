Let's see how
we can help.

Tell us about the person, where they live, and what's going on. We'll put together a Help Action Plan: who to call first, who qualifies, and what to bring.

Takes about 5 minutes with the client on the phone or in person.

What this does

One intake turns into a step-by-step plan, and the plan goes wherever it needs to go.

Takes the whole intake once

Name, date of birth, exact address and county, what's going on, household size, income, income sources, disability and consent, all in one form.

Builds a Help Action Plan

Matches the client to the programs that serve their county and situation, in the order to call them: today, this week, filling the gap, and staying ahead.

Shows who to call and what to bring

Every program comes with its phone number, who qualifies, the documents to have ready, and notes from DRR partners.

Emails the plan as a PDF

Send the full intake to an agency with the client's consent, or a plan-only copy to the client. Or save it as a Gmail draft first.

Saves to Airtable

Creates a record in the Navigator Intakes table with every detail and the plan attached, ready to track follow-up.

Writes the CIL Suite note

A clean Information & Referral note with the client's details and every referral given, ready to paste into CIL Suite.

Say it all at once

Talk instead of typing: tap the microphone on your keyboard and say everything about the person and what's going on. Then press Fill in the form for me.

Where's the microphone?
  • iPhone / iPad: the 🎤 key at the bottom of the keyboard.
  • Android: the 🎤 at the top of the keyboard (Gboard).
  • Windows: press the Windows key + H.
  • Mac: press the Fn (🌐) key twice, or Control twice.
  • Screen reader users: VoiceOver, TalkBack and JAWS dictation all work in this box.
